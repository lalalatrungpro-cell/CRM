# DROPSHIP CRM - PROJECT MANIFESTO & GOALS

## 🎯 What is this Project?
**Dropship CRM 360°** is a specialized, high-performance Customer Relationship & Point-of-Sale Management System designed specifically for **Digital Product Dropshippers** selling software accounts, SaaS subscriptions, and digital keys (e.g., Canva Pro, Google AI / Gemini Advanced, Netflix Premium, ChatGPT Plus, Office 365, etc.).

## 👥 Target Users
- **Primary User**: Shop Owner (Chủ Shop Dropship) managing products, suppliers, profit analytics, and accounting.
- **Secondary Users**: Sales Staff & CSKH handling customer inquiries, quick POS order creation, Zalo text/QR dispatch, and renewal reminders.

## 🚀 Core Value Proposition & Vision
1. **Ultra-Fast Sales Velocity**: Create orders, auto-assign inventory/slots, export VietQR payment cards, and send Zalo confirmations in under 5 seconds.
2. **100% Supplier Traceability**: Track every account back to its wholesale supplier (`supplier_id`) with 1-click warranty/replacement tracking.
3. **Automated Customer Retention & Recurring Revenue**: Daily auto-scanning of account expiration dates (`expire_date`) with pre-built 1-click Zalo renewal prompts to boost customer lifetime value (LTV) by over 40%.
4. **Accounting & Financial Precision**: Standardized 7-column accounting invoices, real-time Net Profit tracking (`Sell Price - Cost Price`), and 2-way debt management (Customer Receivables & Supplier Payables).

## 🏆 Ultimate Goal
To empower digital product dropshippers to **Double Revenues**, **5x Sales Execution Speed**, **Eliminate Customer Churn**, and **Maintain Zero Debt/Warranty Leakage**.
